What we do

Car repairs and servicing, handled end to end.

From a warning light you can't explain to a clutch that's on its way out — we diagnose the real fault, explain it in plain English, and fix it.

Diagnostics & Fault Finding

Warning light on, misfire, or something that just doesn't feel right? Dealer-level diagnostic kit traces the fault properly — no guesswork, no parts thrown at it.

Routine Servicing & Oil Changes

Interim and full servicing using the correct oil and filters for your engine, with the service record stamped to protect your warranty.

Brakes & Suspension

Pads, discs, calipers, shock absorbers, springs and bushes. Shorter stopping distances, steadier handling and a quieter ride.

Clutch & Gearbox Repairs

Slipping clutch, difficult gear selection or a noisy transmission? We replace clutches and repair gearboxes on manual and automatic cars.

Pre-MOT Checks

A thorough pre-MOT inspection so you know exactly what needs doing — and what doesn't — before you pay for a test.

General Mechanical Repairs

Timing belts, water pumps, cooling systems, exhausts, steering and everything in between — all makes and models welcome.
